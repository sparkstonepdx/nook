The dashboard's Server section shows headroom: memory, CPU and disk with a status for each, upgrade advice (add RAM, a faster CPU, more disk, or raise one app's limit), and charts for the last day, week or month. nook records a sample every 5 minutes and notifies you when the status changes. App limits can now be changed after creation from Manage → Limits or `nook-admin limits`, without restarting the app. Dashboard notices are now a line with a status dot instead of boxes.
