Updating nook happens in one place. The banner and the footer both open an update sheet with the release notes and an Install button; there's no browser confirm. While nook restarts, the sheet says so instead of showing the cut-off connection as an error. It waits until the new version answers, then reloads once and says "Updated to nook v…". A failed download or signature check shows its output, and Check now opens the sheet when it finds an update.
